01 / Mixed-signal · High-speed PCB

Custom Phone

A custom CM5-based smartphone PCB bringing cellular, GPS, high-speed interfaces, and power distribution onto a dense six-layer board.

  • 6-layer mixed-signal PCB
  • USB-C · 5 Gbps
  • Ethernet · 1 Gbps
  • Power distribution · up to 7 A

Impedance-controlled, length-matched routing for HDMI, Ethernet, MIPI, PCIe, and USB. Power distribution supports loads up to 7 A with ideal-diode protection for charging from multiple ports.

Ongoing · PCB design complete; enclosure and firmware in progress.

02 / RF · Antenna design · Analog

Handheld Radar System

A handheld 2.4 GHz radar design for distance, velocity, and synthetic aperture radar imaging.

  • 2.4 GHz RF transceiver
  • 2 × 2 antenna array
  • 4th-order active low-pass filter
  • 15 kHz filter cutoff

A VCO and power amplifier feed the transmit path and mixer; an LNA amplifies the receive signal before downconversion. The 2 × 2 antenna array was optimized in ANSYS HFSS for impedance and phase matching.

Design shown · PCB render and schematic.
